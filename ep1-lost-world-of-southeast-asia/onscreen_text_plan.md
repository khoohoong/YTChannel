## THE RULES (how we time text — Bright Side style: clean, brief, never a wall)
1. **A date/year appears the instant the narrator says it**, and holds ~2–3s, then fades.
   Format big numbers the friendly way: **"51,200 years ago"**, **"~20,000 years ago"**, **"800 years ago"**.
2. **A place-name appears when we first cut to that place** (a light lower-third), holds ~2–3s, fades.
   Don't repeat it every time we return — once per place is enough.
3. **Episode title** shows once, in the cold open, over the hero seascape.
4. **Segment titles are optional and minimal** — Bright Side often skips them. If used, keep them to a
   short 1–2s lower-third, not a full title card. (Recommended: use them; see below.)
5. **Map labels** (place-names, routes, "Ice Age vs Today") live on the code-rendered maps —
   added in the editor as overlays, never generated. See `visual_direction.md` MAP1/MAP2/MAP3.
6. Style: single clean sans-serif, subtle drop shadow, lower third or lower-center, safe margins.
   Numbers/dates can be a touch larger for punch. Match the map palette accent (amber `#E0A44E`).

## HOW THE FOOTAGE CARRIES THE SCRIPT (visual = what the words say)
Every shot in `visual_direction.md` is chosen to *show the exact thing the narrator is describing at that
moment*, not generic B-roll. The mapping:
- "sea dropped, one giant piece of land" → **S1-a/S1-b** exposed plains + rivers; **S1-d** the flooding.
- "oldest story… a pig and three figures" → **S2-b** the accurate painting; **S2-c** the hand painting it.
- "tiny human, skull the size of a grapefruit" → **S3-b** scaled hominin; **S3-e** the small skull.
- "boats with a float on the side… read the stars" → **S4-a/S4-b** outrigger canoes; **S4-c** night navigation.
- "biggest city… jungle crept in" → **S5-b** the living city; **S5-e** roots swallowing the temple.
When we edit, each on-screen date/label above lands on the shot that depicts it — so the words, the
picture, and the text all say the same thing at the same second.
