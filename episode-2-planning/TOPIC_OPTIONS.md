# EPISODE 2 — TOPIC OPTIONS (SE Asia only)
### Drafted 2026-07-05. Phase 0 of the playbook: concept lock, no spend.

The user asked for topic suggestions, SE Asia only, open to a 3-episode single-country
mini-series (e.g. Malaysia). Options below, with a clear recommendation.

---

## ⭐ OPTION A (RECOMMENDED) — "MALAYSIA: THE IMPOSSIBLE COUNTRY" — 3-part mini-series

One country, three self-contained films, each with its own Bright-Side-grade hook. A series
multiplies the channel: shared maps/assets, binge-flow between episodes, and a playlist YouTube
can recommend as a unit. Malaysia is the strongest single-country candidate in the region for
hooks-per-episode, and it's home turf for the channel's likely core audience.

| Ep | Working title | The hook | Era |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | **Why Is Malaysia Split Into Two Pieces?** | A nation in two halves, 640 km of sea apart; the merger, Brunei's "no", Singapore's expulsion | 1957–1966 |
| 3 | **Malacca: The City That Ruled the World's Most Important Strait** | A fishing village becomes the choke-point of global trade; Zheng He, the Portuguese conquest 1511, and why the strait still carries ~1/4 of world shipping | 1400–1641 |
| 4 | **The White Rajahs: The Family That Owned a Country** | An English adventurer is given Sarawak by a sultan in 1841 and his family rules it as private kings for a century — one of history's strangest states | 1841–1946 |

- Ep 2 sample script already drafted (`sample_script_why_malaysia_is_split_in_two.md`).
- Massive reuse of the code map renderer (colonial borders, trade routes, the two halves).
- Order note: release modern-first (the split) because it's the strongest title/thumbnail, then
  go back in time. Chronological order (Malacca first) is the alternative.

## OPTION B — Same trilogy, chronological order
Malacca (Ep 2) → White Rajahs (Ep 3) → The Split (Ep 4). Cleaner as a "history of Malaysia"
arc, but leads with the weaker thumbnail and delays the best hook.

## OPTION C — Standalone: "The Empire That Vanished: Srivijaya"
> **UPDATE 2026-07-05:** user asked for a trilogy built on this option — full series plan now in
> `VANISHED_KINGDOMS_TRILOGY.md` (Srivijaya → Champa → Majapahit). That file supersedes the
> standalone framing below.
A maritime empire that dominated SE Asia for 600 years — and was so completely forgotten that
historians only rediscovered it existed in 1918. Strong "lost world" hook, continues Episode 1's
region-wide deep-history lane. Downside: sparse visual record (mostly water, ships, Palembang),
and no series momentum.

## OPTION D — Standalone: "The Strait That Controls the World"
The Malacca Strait today: ~a quarter of global trade through a channel ~2.8 km wide at its
narrowest; piracy, choke-point geopolitics, "what if it closed tomorrow". Very Bright Side, very
thumbnail-able. Downside: current-affairs flavored — dates faster than history episodes, and
overlaps with Option A's Ep 3 (better used as a segment inside it).

---

## RECOMMENDATION
**Option A.** Best hooks, best asset reuse, builds a franchise instead of a one-off, and the
Ep 2 script draft already exists. If the user prefers not to commit to 3 episodes up front:
make Ep 2 ("the split") standalone-safe — it already is — and decide on Eps 3–4 after seeing
its performance.
