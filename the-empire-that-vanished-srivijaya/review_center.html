THE EMPIRE THAT VANISHED — Episode 2 visual review

How to review: play each clip. Anything with a glitch, wrong era, gibberish text, or a broken face — note its label (e.g. "B7 bad") and send the list back. Approved heroes (H1–H5) get upscaled to full HD before the edit. To save a file: right-click the download link → "Save Link As…". Links expire after a while — if one dies, the job ID in assets.md re-fetches it.
