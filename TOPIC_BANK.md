- Mix formats: myth-buster (A), rivalry (B), epic/map-heavy (C), quick-fire listicle (D: "5 foods with fake origin stories").
- Vary length: 8–10 min standard, 12–15 min epics once a month.
- Let analytics pick: after ~8 episodes, double down on whichever category gets the best click-through and retention.
- Shorts from every episode: one "myth vs. fact" per Short.
