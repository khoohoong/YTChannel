## THUMBNAIL CONCEPT

**Design principle:** one striking face/icon + 2–4 huge words + high contrast. Readable at phone size.

### Concept A — "The Face" (recommended)
- **Left 60%:** the *Homo floresiensis* reconstruction bust (asset `S1_floresiensis_bust.png`),
  dramatically lit against near-black, eyes toward camera. Humans-looking-at-faces = high CTR.
- **Right 40%:** the Angkor towers at sunrise (from `H4_angkor_aerial`) as a darker background layer,
  amber gradient blending the two halves.
- **Text:** **"STRANGER THAN YOU THINK"** — 2 lines, heavy condensed sans, off-white with a thin
  amber underline on "STRANGER". Bottom-left.
- **Accent:** small amber location pin + faint map graticule (ties to the map style) top-right.

### Concept B — "The Map Reveal"
- Full-frame the dark teal map (from `MAP4_ocean_spread`) with the glowing amber voyage routes
  fanning across the ocean.
- Big text top-left: **"WHO REALLY SETTLED THE PACIFIC?"**
- A single bright pin on Taiwan. Very on-brand with the map visuals; more mysterious, less face-driven.

### Concept C — "Scale Shock"
- The tiny skull beside the normal human skull (`N1c_skull_scale`), macro, dramatic side light.
- Text: **"A HUMAN THIS SMALL?"** with an arrow. Pure curiosity-gap; strong for the Flores angle.

**My pick:** **Concept A** — a face plus "STRANGER THAN YOU THINK" gives both the emotional pull and
the curiosity gap, and it reuses assets you already have. Palette: near-black ground, off-white text,
amber (#E0A44E) accent — consistent with the film + maps.

**Specs:** 1280×720, keep text clear of the bottom-right (timestamp overlay) and out of the outer
~6% (safe margins). Test legibility by shrinking to ~120px wide — if the words survive, it works.
